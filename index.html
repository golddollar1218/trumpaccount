Answers

Trump Account Fund is a token launching on Robinhood Chain. Trading the token generates fees, and the intent of this project is to periodically claim those fees and donate them into real children's Trump Accounts — the U.S. government program that gives eligible American kids a tax-advantaged investment account. It is an independent project, not a government one.

Every trade carries a 1% pool fee, which the launchpad splits between the token's creator and the platform — the fund receives the creator's 70% share, so roughly 0.7% of trading volume. That share accumulates and is claimed on-chain on a recurring basis, then contributed to children's Trump Accounts through publicly posted contribution links. Each contribution is published here with a link to its confirmation page.

The token can lose value, including all of its value. Buying it is not a donation, it is not tax-deductible, and it gives you no claim on anything the project donates. No one should buy it expecting a return, a payout, or a higher price later. Nothing on this site is financial advice or an offer of an investment. If you buy, treat the money as money you are willing to lose entirely.
